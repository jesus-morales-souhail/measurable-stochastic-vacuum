## What anyone can download

DESI DR2 BAO means and covariances used in the sister repo are public (collaboration releases / Zenodo).  
\(\sigma_8\), \(\Omega_m\), \(n_s\), \(h\) are ordinary Planck-class numbers.  
Buchert, slip, Stage-IV forecast papers are literature.

I do not have private DESI spectra or secret collaboration chains.

## What I actually built

On top of that public material:

1. Residual analysis under an OU/QNM kernel and the ceiling \(\sigma_X<1.5\times 10^{-4}\) (95% CL), plus the soft-amplification gap for a pure Sorkin seed (sister repo).  
2. Explicit check that this residual does not explain \(\sim 8\%\) \(H_0\) at DESI-safe amplitude.  
3. Conditional uniqueness \(\ell_*\sim R_{\mathrm{nl}}\) under axioms A0–A4.  
4. Numbers for \(R_{\mathrm{nl}}\), mask correlation, packing, BBKS peak scale.  
5. Order-of-magnitude \(g_{\mathrm{eff}}\) from a Gaussian averaging proxy, compared afterwards to DESI.  
6. A pre-registered residual–structure test plan and a synthetic mock of the estimators.  
7. Three repos with different jobs, and tests that lock the kinematics.

That is an organised independent programme. It is not “more telescope data than DESI.”
